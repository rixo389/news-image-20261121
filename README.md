A make.fun `news-image-20261121` entry: one image that sums up the world's
news of Saturday 21 November 2026.

## Concept

Not a grid of thumbnails. The whole world is one newspaper front page: a
vintage-cartography world map with the day's five biggest stories pinned to
it as numbered markers, a bold masthead naming the day, and the headlines as
the paper's front-page ticker.

1. **Iran strikes Bahrain** — Iran said it hit two US bases in Bahrain;
   two tankers were struck by projectiles near the Strait of Hormuz.
2. **Missile hits Kryvyi Rih** — a Russian ballistic missile hit
   Zelenskyy's hometown; a Russian cargo ship was left burning after a
   drone attack in the Gulf of Finland.
3. **AI sell-off** — the S&P 500 fell 3.7% and the Nasdaq 5.3% as the AI
   sell-off pushed US stocks into correction territory.
4. **COP31 in overtime** — the UN climate talks in Antalya, Turkey ran
   past their deadline over fossil fuels.
5. **'Chapo Isidro' captured** — Mexican special forces captured FBI
   most-wanted cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores in Sinaloa.

Plus, in the masthead: BepiColombo reaches Mercury after eight years
in space.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the pre-rendered
`entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Both runs write the
exact same bytes.
